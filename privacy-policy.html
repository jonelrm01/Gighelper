Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

GigHelper ("the app") is built to help gig workers track income, expenses, mileage, and vehicle maintenance. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

Data storage — local only

GigHelper stores all of your data — entries, vehicle info, settings, and preferences — directly on your device, using your browser's local storage. There is no server, backend, or database operated by us. We do not receive, see, or have access to your data at any time.

What data the app collects

  • Income, expense, and mileage entries you log (amount, category, date, and optional voice transcript used only to parse the entry)
  • Vehicle profile information you provide (make, model, year, odometer, maintenance history)
  • App preferences (language, app name, chosen categories/platforms)
  • An optional email address, if you choose to provide one for maintenance reminders (stored locally only — not sent anywhere unless a future scheduled-reminder feature is explicitly enabled by you)

None of this data leaves your device. We do not sell, share, transmit, or monetize your data in any way, because we never receive it.

Voice input

Voice entry uses your device/browser's built-in speech recognition (e.g. the Web Speech API). Audio processing for this feature is handled by your device or browser, per its own privacy terms — GigHelper itself does not record, store, or transmit audio.

Diagnostic reports

If you use the "Report a problem" feature, the app assembles a short text report (recent on-device error logs, your app version/settings, and device browser info) and hands it to your device's native share sheet, or offers it as a downloadable file. This report is only sent anywhere if you actively choose to share it — for example, by emailing it to us yourself.

Data deletion

Since all data lives on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data/storage in your browser or device settings, or by uninstalling the app. We recommend exporting a CSV backup (available in the app) before doing so if you want to keep your records.

Children's privacy

GigHelper is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will reflect it. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service, which also covers important disclaimers about maintenance estimates and financial data.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the developer via the contact details listed on the app's Play Store page.
