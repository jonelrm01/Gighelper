Add to your home screen

Install this as an app icon, then launch it by voice through your phone's own assistant.

iPhone (Safari)
1. Tap the Share icon.
2. Tap Add to Home Screen, confirm the name, then Add.
3. Open the Shortcuts app → + → Add Action → "Open App" → choose this app.
4. Tap the shortcut's settings → Add to Siri → record a phrase like "Open GigHelper."
5. Say "Hey Siri, open GigHelper" anytime.

Android (Chrome)
1. Tap the ⋮ menu.
2. Tap Add to Home screen / Install app, confirm the name.
3. Say "Hey Google, open GigHelper" — Assistant can open installed home-screen apps by name.
